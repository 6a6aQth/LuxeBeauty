## Key Changes

Optional Neon Auth sign-up and sign-in. A `CustomerProfile` stores name, email, and the booking phone string. Guests still book with no session. Signed-in checkout writes profile name, phone, and email on the server. `/account` lists upcoming and past successful visits for that phone and shows loyalty progress. Upcoming visits reuse the confirmation ticket. Account reschedule uses the existing once / 24-hour / no extra payment rules and rejects a booking whose phone is not the session phone.

## Architecture Decisions

- Neon Auth holds the password. The app table is `CustomerProfile`, matched by `neonUserId`. No foreign key on `Booking`.
- `getNeonAuth()` stays null until the base URL looks like a real Neon Auth URL and the cookie secret is at least 32 characters. Guest checkout does not call Neon Auth unless `useSession` is set.
- Phone is stored trimmed, exactly as typed. Email on the profile is stored lowercase.
- Visit past/upcoming uses Africa/Blantyre as UTC+2.
- `prisma db push` was run after `DATABASE_URL` was added. The Neon database is in sync with the schema.

## Deviations from SDD

- The formula `6 - (count % 6)` never returns 0. When the next booking is the 30% visit (`(count + 1) % 6 === 0`), the account says "Your next visit is the 30% visit". Four successful bookings still read "2 visits until the 30% visit". A multiple of 6 starts a new cycle and shows 6 visits remaining.
- The header reads the client session on every public page so it can show Sign in or Account. Guest payment, lookup, and webhook routes still do not call Neon Auth.
