## Key Changes

Admin sets each service price in whole MWK. A service with no positive price cannot be taken to payment. At charge time the server sums the current prices, stores `serviceTotal` on the pending booking, and sends that sum to PayChangu. A browser amount is ignored. The 30% loyalty flag is still only a flag.

TNM and Airtel keep mobile-money initialize and the verify poll. The mobile number stays hidden until one of those is selected. Bank Transfer uses `POST /direct-charge/payments/initialize` with `mobile_bank_transfer`, `create_permanent_account` false, and the stored total. The page shows the bank name, account number, account name PayChangu returns, the amount, and the expiry when one is sent. Verify calls `GET /direct-charge/transactions/{charge_id}/details` and confirms only when `data.transaction.status` is `success` and the amount equals `serviceTotal`. Any other bank status stays pending.

Expenditure on `/account`, under Newsletter, sums stored totals for successful bookings on that phone whose Blantyre slot has passed. Upcoming visits, past visits, and the ticket show that booking's stored total. A missing total adds nothing and shows no amount.

## Architecture Decisions

- `paymentMethod` is stored on the booking so the webhook knows whether to call mobile-money verify or the bank details endpoint.
- The one-off bank account is stored on the booking. A refresh can show the same account instead of opening another one.
- Bank Transfer starts when the customer presses Pay with that method selected and the policies accepted. Highlighting the method does not open an account.
- Each selected service id is priced once.
